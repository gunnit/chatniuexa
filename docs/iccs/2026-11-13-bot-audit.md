# ICCS bot ("Ask ICCS") — audit, 13 November 2026

**Scope.** The live bot on italchamber.org.sg (bot `cmn93l00d00d713vkdcnkymzn`), as it
answered before the changes in this PR. **Method.** 8 realistic visitor conversations,
18 visitor messages, written by hand turn by turn (each message written after reading
the previous answer), in English and Italian, sent through the same endpoint, Origin and
Referer as the website widget. Facts were checked against the public pages of
italchamber.org.sg the same day; every member link the bot produced (33) was opened and
checked. Full transcripts: [2026-11-13-sim-transcripts.md](2026-11-13-sim-transcripts.md).

**Not covered.** Real visitor conversations: this environment has no database access.
Run `node --env-file=.env scripts/iccs-stats.mjs --samples` to list what real visitors
asked that the bot could not answer, and send the output for a second pass.

## Findings, most important first

### 1. Member links that open an empty profile (data)
In the Italian answer to a furniture maker ("da dove parto?"), 2 of 7 member links were
invented: `…/corporate/270` and `…/corporate/275` open an empty profile page; the real
pages of M LUXURY Asia and SILAT SRL – Il Giardino di Legno are `/corporate/1432775` and
`/corporate/2473514`. **Why:** the question did not look like a directory request, so
the bot had only part of the directory and filled the gap (with web search on). The
prompt already forbade this. **Fix in this PR:** every answer now passes a server-side
member-link guard: a link into the member pages with an id that is not in the directory
is repaired from the member's name, or turned into plain text; a link whose text is a
member's exact name but whose URL is another member's page is repaired too. A test
replays this exact answer. The prompt now also says "never build or guess a member URL".

### 2. An answer cut off mid-word, saved and shown as if complete (reliability)
"…e [Ermen" — the student's second question ended there after 52 s. **Why:** the
web-search (Responses API) path treated an *incomplete* or *failed* answer as finished
and logged nothing; the output budget (2,048 tokens) is shared with the web-search
calls. **Fix in this PR:** incomplete, failed and error endings are detected and logged
with their reason; the visitor sees "(Answer cut short: type 'continue' for the rest)"
in their language; an empty answer becomes an apology instead of nothing; the budget is
4,096 tokens.

### 3. Asked for the Chamber's board, it listed the ISBC board (data)
"Who else sits on the board?" after a question about the Chamber's President returned
the 9 members of the **ISBC** board. **Why:** only the ISBC board page is in the
knowledge base, not "Board of Directors 2025-2028". **Fix:** prompt rule (the two boards
are different bodies; say so and link /board-directors) and the page is added to the
ingest list. *Unverified:* "Gionata Bosco is the current President" (the ICCS board page
sits behind the site's bot check; he is on the ISBC board as General Manager at VIMAR
Asia Pacific).

### 4. Contact details inconsistent (data)
Membership enquiries were sent to marketing@italchamber.org.sg; another answer gave
singapore@italchamber.org.sg. The website footer lists **+65 6223 0356** and
**singapore@italchamber.org.sg**; marketing@ is the Privilege Program contact. **Why:**
no contact or membership page in the knowledge base, so web search picked arbitrary
pages. **Fix:** official contacts in the prompt; /contact and /become-member added to
the ingest list.

### 5. Essays instead of a conversation (voice, engagement)
"Where do I start?" got 3,630 characters in 44 s: generic consulting steps, a heading,
7 member companies and the Embassy guide. Most answers end without a next step, and
apart from the greeting replies the bot never asked the visitor anything. **Fix:** the
prompt now asks for the answer first, 2-5 sentences, 2-3 Chamber-specific next steps
plus ONE tailoring question for broad requests, an optional natural next step, and full
member lists only when a list is asked for. **Plus** the clickable follow-up questions
(below).

### 6. Greets again in the middle of a conversation (voice)
3 of 10 follow-up replies opened with "Buongiorno!", "Good afternoon." or "Ciao!".
**Fix:** prompt — greet only in the first reply.

### 7. Raw citations, tracking parameters and social media as a source (voice, data)
9 of 18 answers carried links ending in `?utm_source=openai`; 11 appended raw citations
like `([italchamber.org.sg](…))`; ticket prices were cited from a LinkedIn post.
**Fix:** tracking parameters are stripped from every answer server-side; the prompt
(and the generic web-search rule for all bots) asks for one inline link to an official
page and no social media.

### 8. Typos and partial names (data)
"belluzo contact?" got the profile link and "you can use the profile to find the contact
details". **Why:** a one-word lowercase query is not recognised as a member lookup, and
the keyword fallback needs exact spelling. **Partly fixed:** the prompt asks the bot to
match close spellings. **Not done:** fuzzy name matching in retrieval (recommended next).

### 9. "Fashion" lists include chocolate and gym equipment (data)
"Which fashion companies are members?" returned 23 members, including Venchi
(chocolate), Technogym and furniture brands, because they are tagged "Also relevant to:
LUXURY RETAIL". **Fix:** the prompt lets the bot put the closest matches first.
**Recommended:** review the cross-sector tags for LUXURY RETAIL.

### 10. The widget forgot the conversation on every page change (code)
The widget showed the previous chat after the visitor moved to another page of
italchamber.org.sg, but started a new session, so the bot answered follow-ups ("and for
members?") with no memory of them. It also inflated the conversation count. **Fixed:** the
session is stored with the conversation for 24 h; "New conversation" starts a new one.

### 11. Long conversations lost their latest turns (code, all channels)
Every route loaded the *first* 20 messages of a conversation, so after 10 exchanges the
bot never saw the newest ones. On WhatsApp, where a phone number is one conversation
forever, the bot's memory stayed frozen at the first exchanges. **Fixed** for web, share
link and WhatsApp.

### 12. Thumbs up/down on the share link were never saved (code)
The share page (/c/lOLj8UA) read the saved message id before the server had sent it, so
reactions were silently dropped. "Clear chat" also kept the old session. **Fixed.**

## New: clickable follow-up questions

After each answer the bot offers three short next questions in the visitor's language
(e.g. "How can I contact Alifood?", "Which members sell wine?"); one click asks it. They
are generated by a small extra model call after the answer has been shown, so they never
delay it, and they are metered as `suggestions` usage. They appear in the website
widget, on the share link and in the dashboard test chat. Per-bot setting ("Suggest
follow-up questions", off by default); `scripts/update-iccs-prompt.ts` switches it on for
ICCS.
